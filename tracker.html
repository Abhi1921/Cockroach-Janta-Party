Status of the 5 Historic Demands

DEMAND 01 🟡 PIL FILED IN SUPREME COURT

Judicial Post-Retirement Rewards Ban

The movement demands no Chief Justice be granted a Rajya Sabha seat as a post-retirement reward. PIL filed in Supreme Court for a mandatory 5-year cooling-off period.

Target: Supreme Court Constitutional Bench
DEMAND 02 🟡 LAW COMMISSION PETITION

Voter Deletion Prosecution under UAPA

If any legitimate vote is deleted without statutory notice, the CEC shall face criminal prosecution under UAPA. Taking away voting rights is no less than terrorism.

Target: Election Commission & Parliament
DEMAND 03 🔵 DRAFT BILL PRESENTED

50% Mandatory Women Reservation

Women shall receive 50% reservation in Parliament (without expanding seat counts) and 50% of all Central Cabinet positions.

Target: Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha
DEMAND 04 🔴 UNDER INVESTIGATION

Anti-Monopoly Media Licences & Anchor Audit

Cancelling news network licences owned by Ambani & Adani conglomerates and investigating bank accounts of Godi media anchors to restore independent journalism.

Target: Ministry of I&B
DEMAND 05 🟢 LAW REFORM ACCORD

20-Year Ban for Defecting Representatives

Any MLA or MP defecting to another political party after winning shall be barred from contesting elections and holding public office for 20 years.

Target: Anti-Defection Act Amendment
