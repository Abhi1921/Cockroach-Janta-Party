🛡️ CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHT TO ASSEMBLE

Unarmed, Peaceful & Legally Protected

Article 19(1)(b) of the Constitution of India guarantees all citizens the right to assemble peacefully and without arms. Every CJP ground rally is coordinated with local authorities and managed by trained student marshals.

Rally Instructions & Safety Code

PROTOCOL 01

Student ID & National Flag Only

Carry your college ID card / CJP Digital Member Card, water bottle, national tricolor flag, and written demand signs. No offensive items or weapons permitted.

PROTOCOL 02

Volunteer Marshals & Traffic Clearance

Follow instructions of CJP volunteer marshals wearing yellow armbands. Ensure emergency ambulances and public traffic lanes remain clear.

PROTOCOL 03

Cleanliness & Waste Management

Post-assembly sanitation swarms clean the protest site before departure. Leaving plastic litter or damaging public property is strictly forbidden.

PROTOCOL 04

Legal Defense & Emergency Helpdesk

If detained or stopped by police, stay calm and inform CJP Legal Affairs Lead Ratna Singh's helpline. Supreme Court order expunges all peaceful protest FIRs.
