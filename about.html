About CJP.

The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) is a youth-led social movement founded in May 2026 by public relations graduate Abhijeet Dipke.

Origin & History

Originating as a satirical social media challenge responding to courtroom remarks calling unemployed youth 'cockroaches', CJP rapidly grew into India's largest non-partisan youth movement with over 30 million registered members across 28 states & UTs.

The 5 Historic Demands

  1. Ban on post-retirement Rajya Sabha seats for Chief Justices.
  2. Statutory UAPA prosecution for Chief Election Commissioners over deleted voter rolls.
  3. 50% mandatory women reservation in Parliament & Central Cabinet.
  4. Cancellation of corporate media monopoly licences & Godi media anchor audits.
  5. 20-year election ban for defecting MLAs & MPs.
