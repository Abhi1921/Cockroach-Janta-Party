The 5 Core Pillars of the Reservation Hatao Movement

The Reservation Hatao Movement (RHA) advocates for structural educational and employment reforms guaranteeing equal opportunity for all Indian youth:

100% MERITOCRACY

Pure Merit-Based Exams

Evaluation in NEET, JEE, UPSC, and SSC based 100% on individual score without caste cutoff disparities.

ECONOMIC AID

Financial Need Scholarships

Government scholarships and fee waivers provided based on family income verification rather than caste identity.

EXAM INTEGRITY

3rd-Party Server Log Audits

Mandatory independent digital audits of NTA testing servers to eliminate paper leaks and backdoor manipulation.
