Vision & Agenda

Our Movement's Goal

The Cockroach Janata Party is established to represent millions of young citizens who feel unrepresented in traditional politics. We aim to dismantle paper-leak mafias, ensure merit-based public employment, and establish absolute financial transparency in political campaigning.

Explore 5 Pillars
Our Agenda for Reform

The Manifesto.

01

Meritocracy Over Paper-Leak Mafias

Strict fast-track legal trials and mandatory compensation for students impacted by exam cancellations.

02

100% Transparent Political & Electoral Funding

Public open ledger system for all political contributions and complete disclosure of party expenses.

03

Educational System & Exam Recruitment Reforms

Time-bound job calendar implementation with strict penalty clauses for recruitment delay.

04

Youth Employment & Fair Opportunity Guarantee

Direct support for young entrepreneurs, freelancers, and vocational skills development across tier 2/3 cities.

05

Digital Rights, Privacy & Free Speech Protection

Protection of independent journalists, digital creators, and student activists against arbitrary censors.

Frequently Asked Questions

About the Movement

What is Cockroach Janata Party (CJP)?
CJP is a youth-led political movement dedicated to fixing systemic issues in Indian administration, recruitment exams, political transparency, and meritocracy.
Why the name 'Cockroach Janata Party'?
The cockroach symbolizes resilience—it survives despite harsh conditions and attempts to eradicate it. Similarly, the burnt-out youth will survive, adapt, and reclaim their rights.
How is CJP funded?
CJP relies entirely on small, transparent micro-donations from students, citizens, and supporters. We publish transparent financial records.
How can I join or open a chapter in my city/college?
You can register using the contact form below. Our team will guide you through establishing a recognized student chapter or volunteering unit.
