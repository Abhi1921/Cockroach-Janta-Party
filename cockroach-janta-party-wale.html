1. What is Cockroach Janta Party Wale?

Cockroach Janta Party Wale is the popular name for the nationwide collective of students, competitive exam candidates, young professionals, and citizens who belong to the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) movement. The term "Wale" (meaning members/supporters of the movement) represents the millions of young Indians who have united under the viral hashtag #MainBhiCockroach.

Rather than functioning as a standard electoral political party registered with the Election Commission of India (ECI), Cockroach Janta Party Wale operates as a satirical civic pressure group. It utilizes humorous memes, sharp public rants, peaceful sit-ins, and high-impact digital campaigns to force government institutions to address systemic youth grievances like NEET paper leaks, delayed recruitment exams, and judicial bias.

2. What is Cockroach Janta Party (CJP)? Full Form & Origins

  • Full Form: CJP stands for Cockroach Janta Party (sometimes referred to in Hindi media as कॉकरोच जनता पार्टी or तिलचट्टा जनता पार्टी).
  • Founding Date: 16 May 2026 in New Delhi, India.
  • Founder: Abhijeet Dipke (Boston University Public Relations alumnus & political communications strategist).

The party was sparked after disparaging courtroom remarks referring to unemployed youth as "cockroaches." Instead of feeling humiliated, founder Abhijeet Dipke and thousands of young aspirants turned the cockroach into an emblem of unshakeable survival: "Cockroaches survive nuclear blasts; youth will survive corrupt exam systems."

4. The Official Cockroach Janta Party Wale 5-Point Manifesto

Cockroach Janta Party Wale has formulated five binding national demands aimed at reforming public administration, judicial integrity, and democratic elections:

  1. 01. Judicial Cooling-Off Period

    Mandatory 5-year cooling-off ban preventing retiring Supreme Court and High Court judges from accepting political appointments or Rajya Sabha nominations.

  2. 02. Voter Deletion Accountability

    Legal accountability and criminal proceedings for election officials if registered voters' names are unlawfully erased from electoral rolls.

  3. 03. 50% Women's Representation

    Mandatory 50% seat reservation for women in Parliament, State Assemblies, and Central Cabinet portfolios.

  4. 04. Anti-Monopoly Media Reforms

    Preventing corporate oligarch monopolies from owning national news networks to ensure independent public interest journalism.

  5. 05. 20-Year Anti-Defection Ban

    Automatic disqualification and 20-year ban from public office for elected representatives switching parties post-election.
