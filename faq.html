❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Is CJP an officially registered political party with the ECI?

No. CJP is a satirical parody and civic youth movement. It is NOT registered with the Election Commission of India and does not contest elections.

Q2. How did the name 'Cockroach Janta Party' originate?

During a hearing on 15 May 2026, a court remark referred to unemployed youth applicants as 'cockroaches'. Founder Abhijeet Dipke adopted the name satirically as a symbol of youth resilience.
