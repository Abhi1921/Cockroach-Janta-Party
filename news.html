🔥 MAJOR BREAKING ANNOUNCEMENT COVERED BY: NDTV, TOI, HT, DW, ANI, DECCAN CHRONICLE

CJP Announces Peaceful March in Delhi on September 5: India Gate to Delhi Police HQ

Barely four weeks after calling off its protest at Jantar Mantar after Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan’s resignation, the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) has announced a nationwide call for a peaceful protest march in New Delhi on September 5, 2026. The march will begin at India Gate and proceed to the Delhi Police Headquarters over the Centre's failure to fulfill its July 25 commitments, including dropping student FIRs and compensating NEET victims' families. Parents of deceased NEET aspirants will lead the march.
