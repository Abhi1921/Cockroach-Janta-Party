Responsible BitChat Use.

BitChat is CJP's open-source Bluetooth peer-to-peer mesh protocol allowing student delegates to relay emergency updates and rally locations during local internet shutdowns.

📶 OFFLINE MESH RESILIENCE

Zero Cellular Towers Needed · End-to-End Encrypted

When internet services are suspended around protest venues, BitChat relays encrypted messages hop-by-hop via phone Bluetooth radios, ensuring student assemblies remain connected.

BitChat Usage Rules & Standards

STANDARD 01

Emergency Medical & Rally Broadcasts

Use BitChat channels strictly for announcing rally assembly points, medical first-aid locations, lost student delegate alerts, and official CJP updates.

STANDARD 02

Hop-by-Hop Cryptographic Security

All BitChat payloads use Curve25519 public key encryption. Intermediate relay phones pass encrypted packets without reading message contents.

STANDARD 03

Zero Rumor / Panic Spreading

Relaying unverified rumors, fake riot alerts, or malicious misinformation over the mesh channel is prohibited and results in cryptographic ban.

STANDARD 04

Open-Source Code Audit

BitChat protocol source code is maintained by CJP Tech Lead Rohan Deshpande and is open to peer audit by student developers across India.
