👤 FOUNDER & CONVENOR PROFILE

Abhijeet Dipke
Founder of CJP

Abhijeet Dipke is the founder and convenor of Cockroach Janta Party Wale (CJP), India's viral youth-led civic movement. A 30-year-old political communications strategist and alumnus of Boston University (M.S. in Public Relations), Dipke launched CJP on May 16, 2026, turning courtroom rhetoric into a nationwide movement for student rights and institutional accountability.

Background & Movement Origins

Educational Foundation

Abhijeet Dipke earned his Master of Science in Public Relations from Boston University. His academic and professional background in strategic communications gave him deep insights into digital advocacy, media dynamics, and public mobilization.

Turning Slurs Into Movement

When derogatory remarks labeled frustrated youth and aspirants, Dipke embraced the identity of the resilient cockroach — an organism that survives any climate. The Cockroach Janta Party was born to channel youth frustration into concrete policy demands.

Campaign Leadership

Dipke has spearheaded key campaigns, including the NEET exam paper leak audits, Jantar Mantar 2.0 youth marches, 100% meritocracy petitions, and rural primary school infrastructure audits across 28 states.
